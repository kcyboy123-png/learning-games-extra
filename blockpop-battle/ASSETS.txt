생성 프롬프트:
Production game sprite sheet on TRUE TRANSPARENT background. Exactly TWO separate full-body cute high quality folded-paper / faceted ceramic toy mascots, facing camera with slight inward turn. Left half: vivid orange little origami fox, white chest and white tail tip, happy confident closed crescent eyes, waving one paw. Right half: navy little origami penguin, cream white belly, tiny golden beak and feet, friendly happy eyes, waving one flipper. Sophisticated warm studio lighting, crisp polygon folds, soft material highlights, charming for children age8-12, consistent with luxury graphite and champagne arcade puzzle game. Each mascot fits centered inside its own half of a wide 2:1 image with generous transparent padding and absolutely no overlap. Equal visual height, full bodies and all feet/tails visible. No floor, no backdrop, no words, no logos, no frames, no UI, no shadows outside characters. Characters large enough to be legible at 140px. This is an original design, not a known franchise.
